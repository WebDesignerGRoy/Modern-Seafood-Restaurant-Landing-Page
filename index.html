Our Story

From ocean to table, discover the journey behind our passion for exceptional seafood

Sourced from the Sea, Crafted with Passion

Founded in 2018, KRAKEN SEAFOOD KITCHEN began with a simple vision: to bring the freshest, most sustainably sourced seafood to our community. Our journey starts before dawn each day, as our trusted local fishermen venture into the pristine waters of the Pacific Northwest.

What sets us apart is our unwavering commitment to quality and sustainability. We work directly with small-scale fishing operations that share our values, ensuring that every dish we serve not only tastes exceptional but also supports responsible fishing practices.

Our culinary team, led by Executive Chef James Mitchell, transforms these ocean treasures into memorable dining experiences. With over 20 years of expertise in coastal cuisine, Chef Mitchell brings out the natural flavors of the sea through innovative techniques and seasonal ingredients.
